    • 给 KaTeX 添加 Tikzcd 支持

    2026-11-182 分钟Rratic

    项目仓库见于 Tikzcd-KaTeX，使用示例如下：

    \begin{tikzcd}
	X \arrow[r, "f"] \arrow[d, two heads] & Y \\
	\mathrm{coim}(f) \arrow[r, dashed] & \mathrm{im}(f) \arrow[u, hook]
\end{tikzcd}

    $$ \begin{tikzcd} X \arrow[r, "f"] \arrow[d, two heads] & Y \\ \mathrm{coim}(f) \arrow[r, dashed] & \mathrm{im}(f) \arrow[u, hook] \end{tikzcd} $$

    \begin{tikzcd}
	A \arrow[r] \arrow[d] \arrow[dr, phantom, "\ulcorner", very near start]
	& B \arrow[d] \\
	C \arrow[r] & D
\end{tikzcd}

    $$ \begin{tikzcd} A \arrow[r] \arrow[d] \arrow[dr, phantom, "\ulcorner", very near start] & B \arrow[d] \\ C \arrow[r] & D \end{tikzcd} $$

    \begin{tikzcd}
  T
  \arrow[drr, bend left, "x"]
  \arrow[ddr, bend right, "y"]
  \arrow[dr, dashed, "{(x,y)}" description] & & \\
    & X \times_Z Y \arrow[r, "p"] \arrow[d, "q"]
      & X \arrow[d, "f"] \\
    & Y \arrow[r, "g"]
      & Z
\end{tikzcd}

    $$ \begin{tikzcd} T \arrow[drr, bend left, "x"] \arrow[ddr, bend right, "y"] \arrow[dr, dashed, "{(x,y)}" description] & & \\ & X \times_Z Y \arrow[r, "p"] \arrow[d, "q"] & X \arrow[d, "f"] \\ & Y \arrow[r, "g"] & Z \end{tikzcd} $$
